# Winning Post Patterns

What actually clears **100+ engagements** for AI-automation/agency sellers (researched 2026).
Lead with a **quantified result or contrarian claim**, **show the actual build** (named tools, real
numbers), end with a **genuine operator question**. Comments (15+ words ≈ 15x a like) and **saves**
are the levers; carousels hit ~4x text.

6 winning hooks: specific-number/result · contrarian · pain-confession · case-study-result-first ·
unexpected-comparison · resource-list. 5 body skeletons: Case study · Data-contrarian · Build-in-public
carousel · 5-Lessons list · Soft product showcase.

Full swipe file: `content/knowledge-base/winning-post-patterns.md`. The agents ([[The Five Agents]])
now write to this by default. Contrast with the [[The Colouring-in Book|recognition/no-CTA method]] —
use that for inbound-recognition posts, use these for reach + comments + saves.
